Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 8, 2026

1. Overview

Banished is a content filtering and distraction blocker extension designed for school-managed Chromebooks. It helps school IT departments enforce acceptable use policies by detecting and blocking unauthorized proxy tools, circumvention sites, title spoofing, and other non-educational content.

2. Information We Collect

Banished operates primarily on-device, with limited automated reporting strictly for domain safety intelligence:

  • Web History (Block Events Only): When a site policy violation, proxy detection, or blocked page event occurs, the extension logs the triggered URL, domain hostname, and violation category.
  • No General Browsing Tracking: The extension does not track, log, or monitor general browsing activity or history on permitted websites.
  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect student names, email addresses, IP addresses, user IDs, device identifiers, or account credentials. All block telemetry is strictly anonymous.
  • Local Configuration: Policy rules (allow/block lists, feature toggles, exempt domains) are read locally from Google Workspace Admin managed storage.

3. Features and Data Processing

The extension includes the following features:

  • Advanced proxy and circumvention detection
  • Title spoofing protection
  • Admin-controlled allow/block lists
  • Custom block pages with school policy information
  • Optional clipboard protection and anti-AI copy features
  • Console-based proxy signature detection
  • Optional bookmark management tools
  • Automated threat reporting to aggregate domain safety blocklists

4. Permissions Used

  • declarativeNetRequest: For search result cleaning and blocking specific resources.
  • storage: To read school-managed policy settings from Google Admin.
  • tabs: To apply configuration updates and enforce blocking on open tabs.
  • bookmarks: Used only if bookmark management features are enabled by the administrator.
  • <all_urls>: Required to monitor page URLs and content for policy enforcement.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

Logged block data (URLs and detection reasons) is transmitted to compile aggregate domain safety blocklists for participating K-12 school network administrators.

  • Data is never sold, leased, or shared with third-party advertisers or data brokers.
  • Data is used solely for single-purpose security enforcement and threat intelligence in educational settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Banished is intended solely for use in K-12 educational environments on school-managed devices. It complies with COPPA, FERPA, and CIPA guidelines by strictly avoiding the collection of personal information from students.

7. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or the extension's operation, please contact your school or district IT administrator.

This privacy policy applies only to the Banished Chrome extension.
